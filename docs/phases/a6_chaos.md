2. **Doors that pop open** (on P2's door cut, Direction 3): a hard side or corner hit may unlatch a door. P2 cut the two front doors (panel, trim, pane, the door mirror goes with its door) with `CarParts` and hinged them at the A pillar. An open door flaps with speed and cornering, wind, noise and grit come in on that side (the Cabin's exposure, like an open window but worse), the map can be sucked out (the existing wind risk), a loose can may fall out; the seat holder pulls it shut with a key (a hand leaves its work: `CrewRules.hands` gets the job, the body reaches for the handle), it will not latch above a speed or while the car corners hard, so the crew must slow or time it. A door torn off by a second hit stays off until a recovery. Host-authoritative in `Cabin` like the windows, synced, tested; seen from outside and in the mirrors on every peer; the dart of a shock gun passes an open door like an open window.
3. **The hands rule, finished** (GAME_DESIGN §3): `CrewRules.hands` is the one rule for what each seat's hands hold. Audit that nothing bypasses it (gun, cans, windows, wipers, map, dome lamp, door, drink, horn, gestures): one hand per job, two jobs at once are refused or queued with a clear answer to the player (a short HUD line), the driver doing anything but steering steers slower (already for the gun). Tests for every pair that must exclude each other.
4. **Helmet intercom and wind**: the crew talk over an intercom: clear inside the car while it works; with a window or a door open at speed, wind noise masks it; a heavy crash can break it for a while (voice then only by "shouting": quieter and muffled, a squelch as it cuts in and out), fixed by a recovery or after a time. Keep it a simple filter on the local playback of the partner's voice (an `AudioEffect` on a bus of its own); proximity voice to other crews unchanged. The host decides breakage and syncs it; the HUD shows a small intercom state. A fainted player's voice is silent; a shocked player's voice stutters for a moment if cheap. Gestures (A5) are the way through when the intercom is down: say so in the hint line.
5. **Things on the road**: people at the spectator places step back when a car comes at them (a simple rule from the car's path, never under the wheels: no hits on people, the game keeps this harmless) and wave; a marshal with a Turkish flag at the start counts down with the lights; more variety for falling trees and rocks (a second tree model or size, a branch that only scrapes the roof). Animals: their sound far off, and the sheep and cows of `docs/phases/downloads.md` standing in a field behind a wall or a fence, never where a car can come.
6. **Energy drink**: the cans in the glovebox can be drunk (a key) by a conscious seat: one clear effect that is funny and fair (decide: for example it cures the exposure faint risk for a while and gives a caffeine tremble to the hands for a few seconds: the map or the wheel shakes a little), or thrown at the partner to wake them sooner. One rule, pure, host-validated, tested; the glove box lid and the hands already follow a can being taken.
7. **Room modes**: Head to head (default: as now) and Classic (cars start 30 s apart, pure time; results compare times), modifiers the host can toggle: Blind driver (the windshield's dirt never clears: wipers smear only), Silent navigator (navigators cannot use voice, gestures and the horn only), Chaos level (off/normal/wild: density of stage events, door and intercom failures, stone chips). 8. **Awards on the results**: the host counts during the race (one pure class with tests): most shocks given, most shocked, own driver shocked, map lost most, biggest air, most recoveries, longest faint, first into a wrong way, filthiest car (`CarDirt`), most dust eaten (time within 30 m behind another car), glass breaker (stones, kicks, cans), door flapper, cleanest run, best gestures (silent crew). 3-5 funny awards per race with crew names, TR/EN, shown on the results with a small icon. Also a stage-record line. Each is a reason to clip and share.
