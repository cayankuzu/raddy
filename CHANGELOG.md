# Changelog

## Unreleased (Phases A0, A2, A3, A6: solid ground, driving feel, stages 2.0, the shock gun)
- **Narrow rally roads:** 3.6-5.0 m wide (Explore 0.8 m wider, Redline 0.2 m narrower), a gravel shoulder you can use,
  a real ditch beside it and a bank a car can drive back up. Every stage is over 2 km long, with a danger at least every 500 m.
- **A forest like a wall:** trunks from 3 m off the road, undergrowth in the ditch, the inside of every corner screened. You see
  about 60 m of road ahead, so the map and the voice matter.
- **Wrong ways:** four or five junctions per stage, dead-end logging tracks with room to turn and closed roads with barriers deep in
  the track. They look like the road carrying on: only the map and the notes ("Kavşak: Sol 3, düz değil!") tell which way.
- **A real rally around you:** km boards, time control, split, finish and STOP boards, chevrons on the hairpins and caution corners,
  hay bales before the rails, marshal tape, parked cars, benches and places for the crowd at the jumps and splashes.
- **Steering per device:** keys ramp in by speed and come back faster, a gamepad gets a soft centre and full lock at the end of the
  stick, wheels are passed through. Driving assist off / normal / high, gamepad softness and vibration in the settings. Gamepad rumble with the road,
  slides and crashes.
- **A livelier car:** more power (0-100 km/h in 5.4 s on gravel, was 6.3 s) and a handbrake that really swings the rear round a hairpin.
- **Shock gun:** every player carries one (right mouse to aim, left mouse to fire): two darts a stage, refilled at each split, 8 m,
  through an open window or a missing windshield; a hit faints the seat for 6 s, then 4 s of immunity. An aiming driver steers one-handed, an aiming
  navigator folds the map. Room rule: off, rivals only or everyone.
- **Mirrors that really reflect:** the interior mirror and both door mirrors show what is really behind you, live, from
  either seat: the road, the dust, a following car and its headlights, in the right place and the right way round. A hard
  side hit breaks a door mirror (it hangs by its wires, black and cracked); a recovery restores it.
- **Everything in the car works:** real needles for speed, revs, temperature, oil, fuel and volts, sweeping up at the key;
  warning lamps; dials that light up with the headlights; pedals, gear lever and a handbrake that move; tail, brake and
  reversing lights, hazards on a stuck car; a dome lamp (L) to read the map by, which half blinds the driver at
  night; windows that slide into the doors, cracks that look like cracks; the car's own wipers, which clean only where they
  pass; pops and a flame on the overrun; brake discs that glow after really hard use; sun visors that come down for a low sun
  ahead, a glove box that opens when you take a can, a radio that lights with the engine.
- **Water and wet roads** mirror the trees, the sky and the cars (Medium and High).
- **Flags:** every flag is the flag of Turkey, in its exact geometry and 3:2 proportions, waving on the
  crowd's poles, flying from the arches, printed on their banners and on the start's board, and on the badges of the car.
- **Start and finish:** real rally gantries with steel truss towers, a printed banner and concrete blocks;
  the grid stands far enough back to see them. Splits have their own yellow boards.
- **Guard rails:** a corrugated, worn W-beam with real posts and bolts, ends turned into the ground, torn
  and bent where something went through.
- **Roads:** fine loose gravel with stones scattered on it and two packed wheel tracks, on every surface;
  lumpy mud with water standing in the tracks; packed snow without odd marks.
- **Ground and plants:** no more lawn in the forest: leaf litter in patches, hummocks, tufts of grass, ferns
  and new bushes, grass along the road's edge. The desert's bushes were black shapes and are real shrubs now.
- **Sky and light:** clouds in every weather, a warmer shade, soft leaves that let the light through.
- **Car glass:** really clear from the seats, no haze over a dark forest; it mirrors the sky from outside.
- **Paper map:** wrong ways are red dashes crossed out by their junction, the road stands out, the numbers no
  longer lie on top of each other, the sheet no longer runs into the pillar.
- **Clean edges:** the picture has no jagged edges on any graphics level any more (it had no smoothing at all), High also catches
  what is thinner than a pixel, and the road stays sharp far ahead.
- **Rocks in the weather:** snow lies on their ledges, rain darkens them and makes them shine.
- **Door mirrors:** their housings are matt plastic; they used to flash white in the sun like polished metal. They gather a faint film of
  road dirt of their own, which the wipers do not reach.
- **Night and bad weather:** a following car's headlights no longer shine through your bodywork onto the dash and the map.
- **Fixes the reviews found:** the map held up no longer goes through the windshield pillar; the rev counter is the driver's and makes way when
  a door mirror lies under it; the mirrors show the saplings and birches beside the road and no words floating without their boards; reflections in the
  pools no longer break into slivers; spray flies off the car's flanks instead of standing in the cabin; a side window no longer slides out through the door;
  guard rails stand on evenly spaced posts; a recovery no longer cools the temperature gauge; a car rolling back by itself no longer shows reversing lights.
- **Results screen:** the table came out a letter per line and could not be read; now it is a proper table with the medal beside each time.
- **Fixes found by looking at every stage:** posts no longer poke through the middle of the boards; the rock slabs that stuck up from the
  desert like wings are gone, whole boulders sit down on the slopes in their place; a low sun ahead no longer turns the gravel white; fog and dust in the
  mirrors are as dim as what you see through the windshield, and on Low fog and dust look like they do on High.
- **Fixes:** rockfalls start only on ground above the road, recoveries never land on a trunk or a boulder, finished crews get no recovery offer,
  bots dodge only what is next and stay on the road, bot runs never write records, times and medals are compared as the screen shows them.

## 0.2.0 — test build (2026-10-19)
Direction change after the first look: a mix of "Left! Left! LEFT!" (asymmetric co-op, cabin
chaos) and "Half Grip" (driving feel) with a realistic-leaning look, fixed named stages instead of
generated road networks.

- **Stages:** six named point-to-point stages (two per biome) with crests, jumps, dips, water
  splashes, cliffs with steel rails, canyon walls and switchbacks; the land follows the road; you
  can leave the road but invisible walls 36 m out keep you on the stage. Start line, two splits,
  finish; places by distance. The branching networks, shortcuts and laps are gone.
- **Pace notes and map:** real rally calls ("Sol 3", "Saç tokası sağ", "Kör tepe!", "Atlama!",
  "Su geçişi!", "Uçurum solda!"); the paper map turns long stages sideways and marks the hazards.
- **Car:** a real rally hatchback model (CC BY) with cockpit, turning steering wheel, clear glass,
  team paint, brake/reverse lamps and weather headlights.
- **Forest:** firs, pines and birches (CC BY) beside the road, a thick edge band and a backdrop.
- **Sound:** engine from a real Toyota Corolla rally car recording on a five-speed sound gearbox,
  gravel crunch, squeal, water, wind, crashes, horn (H), rain and thunder, paper map, menu clicks.
- **HUD:** stage bar with every crew's car, split times with the gap to the fastest, rev counter,
  gear and speed. Mouse free look in the cockpit.
- **Glass:** mud, cracks and two wiper blades on the real windscreen.
- **Water splashes** slow the car, soak the glass and splash open-window seats.
- **Stage surprises:** a fir falls across the road in front of the first crew (squeeze past the
  trunk or push through the crown and lose the view to pine needles), boulders roll down canyon and
  mountain banks.
- **Damage you can see:** the bonnet pops, flaps up at speed until the driver is blind and then
  tears off; bumpers hang and fall off; the engine steams, then smokes. Crashes jolt the camera.
- **Records and medals:** best time per stage on this PC with the crew's names; bronze, silver and
  gold times shown in the room and on the results.
- **Performance:** scenery batched by mesh (forest draw calls 1429 → 608); graphics quality
  setting (Auto/Low/Medium/High; Auto picks Low on integrated graphics).
- **Fixes:** the grid sat behind the stage's start wall; jump landings counted as crashes (drivers
  now report body impacts to the host); the model's dark glass darkened the cockpit view; critical
  damage kept closing the recovery offer while the car crawled; terrain and walls near hairpins;
  gearbox limiter and clutch take-up; HUD/map texts in both languages.

## 0.1.0 — first playable (2026-10-12)
Driving prototype with ray-cast suspension and surfaces; host-authoritative rooms for six players
in three crews with room codes and UPnP; proximity voice chat; navigator's paper map with pace
notes; windows, windscreen, dirt, wipers, exposure and faints; cans thrown from the cabin; three
damage levels; joint R recovery; main menu, room, race, results, pause and settings (TR/EN);
automated unit, network and end-to-end flow tests.
