# Changelog

## Unreleased (Phases A0, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6: solid ground, driving feel, stages 2.0, effects, the crew, the shock gun)
- **A crew in the car:** every seat a player holds now has somebody in it: a driver and a navigator in race suits of the team
  colour with white stripes, dark gloves and boots, full-face helmets and six-point harnesses. You see your own arms on the wheel,
  your knees and your harness, your partner beside you and in the interior mirror, the rival crews through their windows.
- **Hands at work:** the driver's hands ride the steering wheel as it turns (at full lock the rim slides through them, as a rally
  driver pushes and pulls), go to the gear lever on a shift, pull the handbrake, press the horn, flick the wipers and wind the window;
  the navigator holds the map up by its edges and rests the hands on the knees when it is folded. Raise the shock gun and it is in
  your hand, its darts leave from it. Heads turn where their players look; a fainted crew member slumps in the harness, a shock makes
  them jerk first, and every screen sees a can thrown out of the window and a windshield kicked out.
- **Hand signals:** left, straight on, right or "slow down" (1-4 or the d-pad, rebindable), each a sign of its own made against the
  windshield where your partner sees it without looking away from the road: a finger across to their side, a thumb for the other side,
  a chop for straight on, a pat for slow down. The backs of the gloves are in the suit's colour with its stripe and after dark the lit
  dials light your hands, so a crew can talk without a word, by night too. The navigator's map folds for it.
- **One hand on the wheel:** the driver now steers one-handed (less lock, slower) not only with the shock gun up but also while
  signalling, winding the window, flicking the wipers or pressing the horn, and no better with both hands off it.
- **Sitting like a rally crew:** the seat is lower, upright and close to the wheel and the view narrower, so the road fills half of
  the screen instead of a third under a black roof; the mirrors are in view or a glance away, the dials read through the wheel, which
  sits a little higher. You turn your head as far as the bucket seat's wings let you.
- **A rally car's cabin:** a roll cage with foam where a head could hit it, bucket seats in a racing mesh fabric, a trip meter in
  front of the navigator and a fire extinguisher; the rear seat and the sun visors are gone (the cage runs where they hung).
- **Heads that feel the car:** the harness holds the crew in their seats and their necks give: your partner's helmet leans out of a
  corner, nods under braking and jolts on a landing or a hit, on every screen, in your own car and in your rivals'. The navigator's
  kick now goes over the dashboard and the windshield goes when the soles hit it; a can flies when it leaves the hand.
- **A rally car's livery:** every car carries its crew's number on a panel on each front door, on a rally plate on the bonnet and on
  the rear window, the crew's names after a small flag of Turkey on the rear side windows, and a mud flap behind each wheel. The road
  covers all of it as it covers the paint.
- **A crowd at the stages:** people with faces stand behind the tape at the start, at the finish and at the places for spectators,
  dressed for the weather (t-shirts and caps in the desert, jackets and beanies in the mountains), some with a small flag of Turkey.
  They watch a car come, cheer, clap, wave and jump as it goes by, and settle when it has gone; a car standing still gets their eyes and
  no more.
- **Times of day:** the room picks day, golden hour, dusk, night or Random, like the weather. The golden hour is a low warm
  sun you drive into. Dusk is the blue hour: the sun has set, the sky still burns orange and violet
  and the land lies in its blue light, the road readable before the headlights reach it. At night there is a cold moon and stars on a clear night and nothing at all under clouds: you see only what
  your headlights reach.
- **Headlights that reach:** a real lamp's beam, brightest just under the horizon, lights the road far ahead and little
  above it; the trees by the road catch it, a corner beyond the beams stays dark. Fog and dust glow in them, and behind
  another car in its dust at night you see nothing but the glowing cloud. A car close behind you lights the trees round you,
  never your road or your windshield through your car: your own shadow lies ahead of you. The dials light up, after dark tail lights leave a faint red
  on the road behind and brake lights a bright one, a follower's beams light up your mirrors. On High your own headlights cast shadows.
  A flag in your beams stays red with its crescent and star, a guard rail a grey rail, where they burned to a white glare that hid the
  road behind them.
- **A light pod for the night:** at dusk and at night every car carries a pod of four lamps on its bonnet, its cover plate in the team
  colour. The two spot lamps throw their light far down the road, the two wide ones light the sides of a corner. Night stays hard, a corner
  still shows late behind its trees, but you can drive it. A loose bonnet points the pod at the sky; a bonnet torn off takes it away, dark.
- **Dirt on the windshield in the colour of the road:** tan dust in the desert, brown mud in the forest, grey slush in snow, and a few small
  flecks rather than a swarm. It is a haze the light comes through: driving into a low sun it glows, as a real dusty windshield does. The
  wipers clear what they sweep; the door mirrors gather a little of the same dirt.
- **Snow on the trees, rain on the leaves:** in snow the firs carry it in broken clumps on top of their branches and the undergrowth is white
  long before; rain darkens the leaves and makes them shine.
- **A forest floor:** under the trees fallen needles, leaves and moss lie in the shade of the crowns instead of a lawn, and the shade on the ground
  keeps the ground's own colour instead of a blue-grey sheen of the sky.
- **Rain, snow and dust storms:** round snowflakes, soft wisps of blowing dust and raindrops that streak; nothing of the weather drifts through the
  cabin or past your eyes.
- **Dust that hangs in the air:** on dry gravel, dirt and sand a car drags a real cloud that lingers for seconds, drifts on the wind, is darker in the shade of
  the trees and glows when the sun or the headlights are behind it. Two seconds behind another car you lose the road: the navigator is all you have.
- **Snow, mud and water:** snow throws a plume of powder, mud and wet roads throw clods and spray instead of dust, water leaves the flanks in sheets, and a hard
  landing on dry ground blows a ring of dust out from under the car.
- **Stones:** the rear wheels throw stones that bounce away, and you hear them ping under the floor.
- **A stone on the windshield is an event now:** a stone leaves a small bullseye with a sharp tick (a dark pit and a half moon of crushed glass), the next one cracks the glass. It is rare on your own (about one run in
  ten cracks) and the price of following someone close through their stones on loose ground.
- **A car that gets dirty:** dust climbs from the sills and fans out behind the wheels, mud flies up in drops that run together, snow packs on low down; the
  paint and the team colour show through at the start, by the finish of a dry stage the car is filthy. A water splash rinses the lower half; a recovery cleans nothing.
- **Tyre marks:** every tyre leaves its mark for the rest of the race: pressed gravel, deep ruts in mud and snow, a furrow in sand, wide swept arcs where a car
  slid. The crew behind drives over the leader's line.
- **A crack that looks like a crack:** hair-thin straight lines run out from a small star where the stone hit, with a branch here and there; every stone after
  sends them further and leaves a little star of its own. They flare when the light catches them, most of all driving into a low sun. Side windows crack the same way.
- **Bigger hits:** a crash throws sparks off rocks and rails, bark and a shower of leaves, sprigs and dry needles off a tree that comes down past the windshield (snow in winter), splinters off wood, straw off a
  bale, dust off the ground; a side window that breaks drops its glass.
- **Narrow rally roads:** 3.6-5.0 m wide (Explore 0.8 m wider, Redline 0.2 m narrower), a gravel shoulder you can use,
  a real ditch beside it and a bank a car can drive back up. Every stage is over 2 km long, with a danger at least every 500 m.
- **A forest like a wall:** trunks from 3 m off the road, undergrowth in the ditch, the inside of every corner screened. You see
  about 60 m of road ahead, so the map and the voice matter.
- **Wrong ways:** four or five junctions per stage, dead-end logging tracks with room to turn and closed roads with barriers deep in
  the track. They look like the road carrying on: only the map and the notes ("Kavşak: Sol 3, düz değil!") tell which way.
- **A real rally around you:** km boards, time control, split, finish and STOP boards, chevrons on the hairpins and caution corners,
  hay bales before the rails, marshal tape, parked cars, benches and places for the crowd at the jumps and splashes.
- **Steering per device:** keys ramp in by speed and come back faster, a gamepad gets a soft centre and full lock at the end of the
  stick, wheels are passed through. Driving assist off / normal / high, gamepad softness and vibration in the settings. Gamepad rumble with the road,
  slides and crashes.
- **A livelier car:** more power (0-100 km/h in 5.4 s on gravel, was 6.3 s) and a handbrake that really swings the rear round a hairpin.
- **Shock gun:** every player carries one (right mouse to aim, left mouse to fire): two darts a stage, refilled at each split, 8 m,
  through an open window or a missing windshield; a hit faints the seat for 6 s, then 4 s of immunity. An aiming driver steers one-handed, an aiming
  navigator folds the map. Room rule: off, rivals only or everyone.
- **Mirrors that really reflect:** the interior mirror and both door mirrors show what is really behind you, live, from
  either seat: the road, the dust, a following car and its headlights, in the right place and the right way round. A hard
  side hit breaks a door mirror (it hangs by its wires, black and cracked); a recovery restores it.
- **Everything in the car works:** real needles for speed, revs, temperature, oil, fuel and volts, sweeping up at the key;
  warning lamps; dials that light up with the headlights; pedals, gear lever and a handbrake that move; tail, brake and
  reversing lights, hazards on a stuck car; a dome lamp (L) to read the map by, which half blinds the driver at
  night; windows that slide into the doors, cracks that look like cracks; the car's own wipers, which clean only where they
  pass; pops and a flame on the overrun; brake discs that glow after really hard use; a glove box that opens when you take a can,
  a radio that lights with the engine.
- **Water and wet roads** mirror the trees, the sky and the cars (Medium and High).
- **Flags:** every flag is the flag of Turkey, in its exact geometry and 3:2 proportions, waving on the
  crowd's poles, flying from the arches, printed on their banners and on the start's board, and on the badges of the car.
- **Start and finish:** real rally gantries with steel truss towers, a printed banner and concrete blocks;
  the grid stands far enough back to see them. Splits have their own yellow boards.
- **Guard rails:** a corrugated, worn W-beam with real posts and bolts, ends turned into the ground, torn
  and bent where something went through.
- **Roads:** fine loose gravel with stones scattered on it and two packed wheel tracks, on every surface;
  lumpy mud with water standing in the tracks; packed snow without odd marks.
- **Ground and plants:** no more lawn in the forest: leaf litter in patches, hummocks, tufts of grass, ferns
  and new bushes, grass along the road's edge. The desert's bushes were black shapes and are real shrubs now.
- **Sky and light:** clouds in every weather, a warmer shade, soft leaves that let the light through.
- **Car glass:** really clear from the seats, no haze over a dark forest; it mirrors the sky from outside.
- **Paper map:** wrong ways are red dashes crossed out by their junction, the road stands out, the numbers no
  longer lie on top of each other, the sheet no longer runs into the pillar.
- **Clean edges:** the picture has no jagged edges on any graphics level any more (it had no smoothing at all), High also catches
  what is thinner than a pixel, and the road stays sharp far ahead.
- **Rocks in the weather:** snow lies on their ledges, rain darkens them and makes them shine.
- **Door mirrors:** their housings are matt plastic; they used to flash white in the sun like polished metal. They gather a faint film of
  road dirt of their own, which the wipers do not reach.
- **Night and bad weather:** a following car's headlights no longer shine through your bodywork onto the dash and the map.
- **Fixes the reviews found:** the map held up no longer goes through the windshield pillar; the rev counter is the driver's and makes way when
  a door mirror lies under it; the mirrors show the saplings and birches beside the road and no words floating without their boards; reflections in the
  pools no longer break into slivers; spray flies off the car's flanks instead of standing in the cabin; a side window no longer slides out through the door;
  guard rails stand on evenly spaced posts; a recovery no longer cools the temperature gauge; a car rolling back by itself no longer shows reversing lights.
- **Rev counter:** it no longer goes nearly invisible whenever the car is moving; it steps aside only when the right door mirror is really under it.
- **A dirty windshield looks dirty:** a film of dust with the runs the wind draws and small specks of mud, instead of big brown blotches like camouflage; at its worst you can only guess the road.
- **Pools:** the trees mirrored in the water are whole, not cut into bands.
- **Results screen:** the table came out a letter per line and could not be read; now it is a proper table with the medal beside each time.
- **Fixes found by looking at every stage:** posts no longer poke through the middle of the boards; the rock slabs that stuck up from the
  desert like wings are gone, whole boulders sit down on the slopes in their place; a low sun ahead no longer turns the gravel white; fog and dust in the
  mirrors are as dim as what you see through the windshield, and on Low fog and dust look like they do on High.
- **Fixes:** rockfalls start only on ground above the road, recoveries never land on a trunk or a boulder, finished crews get no recovery offer,
  bots dodge only what is next and stay on the road, bot runs never write records, times and medals are compared as the screen shows them.

## 0.2.0 — test build (2026-10-19)
Direction change after the first look: a mix of "Left! Left! LEFT!" (asymmetric co-op, cabin
chaos) and "Half Grip" (driving feel) with a realistic-leaning look, fixed named stages instead of
generated road networks.

- **Stages:** six named point-to-point stages (two per biome) with crests, jumps, dips, water
  splashes, cliffs with steel rails, canyon walls and switchbacks; the land follows the road; you
  can leave the road but invisible walls 36 m out keep you on the stage. Start line, two splits,
  finish; places by distance. The branching networks, shortcuts and laps are gone.
- **Pace notes and map:** real rally calls ("Sol 3", "Saç tokası sağ", "Kör tepe!", "Atlama!",
  "Su geçişi!", "Uçurum solda!"); the paper map turns long stages sideways and marks the hazards.
- **Car:** a real rally hatchback model (CC BY) with cockpit, turning steering wheel, clear glass,
  team paint, brake/reverse lamps and weather headlights.
- **Forest:** firs, pines and birches (CC BY) beside the road, a thick edge band and a backdrop.
- **Sound:** engine from a real Toyota Corolla rally car recording on a five-speed sound gearbox,
  gravel crunch, squeal, water, wind, crashes, horn (H), rain and thunder, paper map, menu clicks.
- **HUD:** stage bar with every crew's car, split times with the gap to the fastest, rev counter,
  gear and speed. Mouse free look in the cockpit.
- **Glass:** mud, cracks and two wiper blades on the real windscreen.
- **Water splashes** slow the car, soak the glass and splash open-window seats.
- **Stage surprises:** a fir falls across the road in front of the first crew (squeeze past the
  trunk or push through the crown and lose the view to pine needles), boulders roll down canyon and
  mountain banks.
- **Damage you can see:** the bonnet pops, flaps up at speed until the driver is blind and then
  tears off; bumpers hang and fall off; the engine steams, then smokes. Crashes jolt the camera.
- **Records and medals:** best time per stage on this PC with the crew's names; bronze, silver and
  gold times shown in the room and on the results.
- **Performance:** scenery batched by mesh (forest draw calls 1429 → 608); graphics quality
  setting (Auto/Low/Medium/High; Auto picks Low on integrated graphics).
- **Fixes:** the grid sat behind the stage's start wall; jump landings counted as crashes (drivers
  now report body impacts to the host); the model's dark glass darkened the cockpit view; critical
  damage kept closing the recovery offer while the car crawled; terrain and walls near hairpins;
  gearbox limiter and clutch take-up; HUD/map texts in both languages.

## 0.1.0 — first playable (2026-10-12)
Driving prototype with ray-cast suspension and surfaces; host-authoritative rooms for six players
in three crews with room codes and UPnP; proximity voice chat; navigator's paper map with pace
notes; windows, windscreen, dirt, wipers, exposure and faints; cans thrown from the cabin; three
damage levels; joint R recovery; main menu, room, race, results, pause and settings (TR/EN);
automated unit, network and end-to-end flow tests.
